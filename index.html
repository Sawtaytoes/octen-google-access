Google account access

Horizon and Mail Sifter are privately hosted applications. You choose whether to connect a Google account to each one.

The applications

Horizon

Shows events from the Google calendars you connect, and lets you create, edit, and delete events on calendars you can already edit. It never changes calendar sharing or deletes a calendar.

Mail Sifter

Helps you read, organize, compose, and send mail. With your permission, it can use Gmail and read Google Contacts to suggest recipient addresses and maintain contact names and email addresses. It also supports IMAP and SMTP accounts.

Your choice and control

Horizon and Mail Sifter have separate Google OAuth clients. The clients share a Google Cloud project and consent identity. Each application requests its own feature permissions and stores its own account tokens. Each account holder authorizes a connection. You can remove access in Google Account settings.

The applications store connected-account tokens and working data on the operator’s TrueNAS system. The original Gmail, Calendar, and Contacts data remains with Google. This public website does not receive your connected-account data.
